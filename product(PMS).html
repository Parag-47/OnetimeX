What is PMS?

Portfolio Management Service (PMS) is a professional financial solution designed to manage investments on behalf of high-net-worth individuals (HNIs) and institutional investors. It offers personalized strategies tailored to financial goals, risk tolerance, and market opportunities.

PMS provides expert-driven asset allocation, continuous monitoring, and active risk management to optimize portfolio performance. With a dedicated fund manager, investors gain access to exclusive market insights and strategic investment opportunities.

Customized Investment Strategy

Expert Fund Management

Managed by seasoned professionals ensuring optimal asset allocation.

Active Portfolio Monitoring

Continuous tracking and adjustments to maximize returns.

Higher Return Potential

Aims to outperform traditional investment avenues.

How PMS Works

Investor Onboarding – Investors choose PMS based on financial goals and risk profile.

Portfolio Customization – A personalized investment strategy is created based on investor preferences.

Active Portfolio Management – Fund managers monitor, rebalance, and optimize investments.

Performance & Reporting – Regular updates and reports ensure transparency for investors.

SEBI Regulation & Compliance

PMS operates under SEBI’s Portfolio Managers Regulations, 2020, ensuring transparency, risk management, and investor protection. SEBI mandates registration, minimum investment criteria, and periodic disclosures to safeguard investor interests.

Types of PMS

Discretionary PMS

The portfolio manager makes investment decisions on behalf of the investor.

Non-Discretionary PMS

The investor makes final decisions, while the portfolio manager provides recommendations.

Advisory PMS

Portfolio managers provide tailored investment advice, but execution is done by the investor.
