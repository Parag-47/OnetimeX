What is AIF?

Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) are privately pooled investment vehicles that collect funds from investors and invest in alternative asset classes beyond traditional stocks and bonds. They are ideal for High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNIs) and institutional investors seeking diversification and high returns.

Regulated by SEBI under the Alternative Investment Funds Regulations, 2012, AIFs provide investors access to exclusive financial opportunities.

Features of AIFs

Exclusive Investments

Available only to HNIs and institutional investors.

Diversified Portfolio

Expert Fund Management

Long-Term Wealth Growth

Focuses on capital appreciation over time.

How AIFs Work

Investors contribute funds into the AIF pool.

Fund Managers strategically invest in alternative assets.

Returns are generated and distributed among investors.

SEBI Regulated for transparency and investor protection.

SEBI Regulation & Compliance

AIFs operate under SEBI's Alternative Investment Funds Regulations, 2012, ensuring structured and legal investment practices. Investors can access SEBI-approved fund managers, promoting safety and accountability.
